Privacy Policy - 150 Club

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Overview

150 Club is a private health and aging radar for personal wellness tracking. It helps you understand trends such as biological age estimates, aging rate, Apple Health data, home health readings, lab report markers, and family health signals.

This policy explains what information 150 Club handles, why it is used, and how you can delete your account and data.

150 Club is not a medical device, diagnosis tool, or replacement for professional medical care.

Information we collect or process

Account information

150 Club may collect account identifiers, email address, authentication provider details, display name, subscription state, app settings, and related account metadata needed to operate the service.

Profile and onboarding information

150 Club may collect information you provide during setup, such as age or date of birth, sex, region, family role, wellness goals, perceived age, and other responses used to personalize your experience.

Apple Health data

If you choose to connect Apple Health, 150 Club reads only the health categories you approve. Depending on your permissions, this may include activity, sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, cardio fitness, body measurements, respiratory metrics, blood oxygen, and related wellness data.

You control Apple Health permissions through iOS Settings and the Apple Health app. You can revoke access at any time.

Home device readings

150 Club may store health readings that you manually enter or capture from home devices, such as blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, scales, body fat scales, thermometers, and pulse oximeters. When you scan a device screen, an image may be processed to extract numbers. You should review and confirm values before saving.

Lab reports and uploaded files

If you upload a lab report or health document, 150 Club may process the file to extract health markers, dates, units, and related information. Uploaded files and extracted markers may be stored with your account so the app can show trends over time.

Family member information

If you use family features, 150 Club may store family member names or nicknames, relationship labels, age or date of birth, sex, health readings, lab markers, risk notes, invitation state, and related family health radar data you choose to enter or share.

Camera, photos, and files

Camera, photo, and file access are used only when you choose actions such as scanning a home device screen or uploading a lab report. 150 Club does not access your camera, photos, or files unless you grant permission or select content.

Device and technical data

150 Club may process basic technical data such as app version, platform, device type, diagnostics, crash information, request logs, and server logs to maintain reliability, security, and abuse prevention.

How we use information

  • To operate core features, including aging radar, biological age estimates, aging rate, trend views, family health views, and data source management.
  • To sync approved Apple Health data and show health trends over time.
  • To save readings from home devices, lab reports, self-tests, and manual entries.
  • To generate wellness insights, early signal alerts, and non-diagnostic trend summaries.
  • To manage accounts, subscriptions, support, privacy requests, and account deletion requests.
  • To maintain app reliability, security, debugging, and abuse prevention.

150 Club does not sell your personal information. 150 Club does not use your health information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

AI and automated analysis

150 Club may use automated systems or AI services to help read device screens, extract lab report markers, summarize trends, or generate wellness insights. These outputs may be imperfect and should be reviewed by you. They are not medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency advice.

Third-party services

150 Club uses service providers to operate the app, including backend hosting, authentication, database storage, file storage, server functions, app distribution, payments, diagnostics, and AI processing where applicable.

These providers process information only as needed to provide services for 150 Club. Apple services may be used for Apple Health permissions, Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution, subscriptions, and related platform functionality.

Sharing

150 Club does not publicly share your health data by default. If you use family features, you choose what family information to enter or connect. If you export or share a report, card, screenshot, or file, that sharing is controlled by you and the destination app or person you choose.

Data retention and deletion

150 Club keeps account data only as long as needed to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, or until you delete your account.

You can delete your account inside the app: Profile - Settings - Delete account. This deletes your 150 Club cloud account and associated cloud account data. If your device is offline or the server cannot be reached, 150 Club shows an error and does not claim deletion succeeded.

You can also contact us at hello@hemiscend.com for account deletion or privacy requests.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled by 150 Club. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

150 Club is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through 150 Club, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when 150 Club changes or when legal, platform, or operational requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
