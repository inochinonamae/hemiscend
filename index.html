About

Hemiscend is an independent iOS studio building apps for people whose minds don't fit the standard mold — whether that's how they visualise, how they organise, or how they think.

Every app starts from a real problem. We care about craft: the details in the interaction, the honesty in the copy, and the respect for the person using it.

Based in Canada. Founded in 2025.
