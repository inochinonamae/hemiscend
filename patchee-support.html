Frequently asked questions

What is Patchee?

Patchee is a personal canvas where you build your own dashboard — drag boxes (tasks, habits, notes, a clock, toys and more) onto a board and arrange them to fit your day.

How do I add a box?

Tap the joystick dot at the bottom of the screen, drag up, and pick a box from the panel. Hold a box's corner to move or resize it.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device. No account is required and we never see your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Back Up Everything to export all your pages, boxes, notes, tasks and routines as a single file you can save anywhere.

I found a bug or have an idea

Please tell us — email hello@hemiscend.com or use Settings → Feedback in the app. Including your device model and app version (Settings → About) helps us fix things faster.
