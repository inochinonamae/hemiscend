Account deletion

You can delete your 150 Club account inside the app:

Open 150 Club - Profile - Settings - Delete account

This deletes your 150 Club cloud account and associated cloud account data. If your device is offline or the server cannot be reached, 150 Club shows an error and does not claim deletion succeeded.

You can also contact hello@hemiscend.com for account deletion help.

Frequently asked questions

What is 150 Club?

150 Club is a private health and aging radar. It helps you track biological age estimates, aging rate, Apple Health trends, home health readings, lab report markers, and family health signals in one place.

Is 150 Club medical advice?

No. 150 Club is not a medical device, diagnosis tool, or replacement for a doctor. The app is designed for personal wellness tracking and early signal awareness. Always speak with a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions, diagnosis, or treatment.

Why does 150 Club ask for Apple Health access?

Apple Health access lets 150 Club read health categories you approve, such as activity, sleep, heart, body, respiratory, or related wellness metrics. You control these permissions in iOS Settings and Apple Health.

Why does 150 Club ask for camera access?

Camera access is used when you choose to scan a home device reading, such as a blood pressure monitor, glucose meter, scale, or pulse oximeter screen. You should always confirm the extracted values before saving them.
