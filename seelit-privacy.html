Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026

Overview

SEELIT is operated by Hemiscend ("we", "us", "our"). SEELIT is a mental imagery training app that helps you strengthen your visualization skills through guided practice exercises.

This policy explains what data SEELIT collects, why we collect it, and your rights over that data.

Data We Collect

Account Data

  • Email address (for account login and account recovery)
  • Apple ID identifier (if you sign in with Apple)
  • Display name (optional, only if you choose to provide one)

Practice Data

  • Your responses to imagery training exercises
  • Vividness scores and practice session results
  • Progress over time across the different imagery modalities
  • Drawings and recall responses you create during exercises

Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and crash reports (for debugging)
  • Anonymous usage analytics (which screens you visit, which exercises you complete)

Subscription Data

  • Subscription status (active, trialing, expired)
  • Transaction history (managed by Apple via the App Store)

Why We Collect This Data

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To save your training progress across devices
  • To show you personalized progress charts and recommendations
  • To process your subscription and verify access to premium features
  • To improve the app based on aggregated usage patterns
  • To respond to your support requests

We never sell your personal data. We never use your practice data for advertising.

How We Protect Your Data

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
  • Database access requires authentication and is logged
  • Data is stored on Supabase servers (Northeast Asia region)
  • Only authenticated users can access their own data (row-level security enforced)

Third Parties We Share Data With

  • Supabase — encrypted database and authentication backend
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple (if used), App Store subscription processing
  • RevenueCat — subscription status verification and entitlement management. Receives only your anonymous user ID and subscription state, not your email or practice data.

We do not share your data with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms beyond what is necessary to run the service.

Your Rights

  • Access — view the data we hold about you
  • Delete — permanently delete your account and all associated data, directly from within the app (Profile → Delete Account)
  • Export — request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Correct — update or correct your account details
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, by deleting your account

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@hemiscend.com. We respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

  • While your account is active: we retain your practice records so you can see your long-term progress.
  • When you delete your account: all your personal data and practice records are permanently deleted from our systems. Aggregated, anonymous statistics may be retained for app improvement.

Children's Privacy

SEELIT is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in-app or by email before they take effect. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
